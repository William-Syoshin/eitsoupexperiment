Fig. 4.  Block Diagrams of Control Architectures.  (a) Open-loop control. The controller drives the plant with dosage signal Ctotal based solely on the reference σref; the output σmeas is not fed back and cannot correct for disturbances or parameter drift. (b) Conventional PID feedback control. The controller computes a fixed-gain proportional–integral–derivative output from the tracking error e = σref − σmeas and drives the plant with dosage signal Ctotal. Performance degrades when plant parameters shift due to environmental changes. (c) Self-Tuning Regulator (STR). The RLS Estimator receives σmeas and Ctotal to produce real-time estimates α̂ and β̂ (forgetting factor λ = 0.98). The Gain Adjustment block then rescales Kp, Ki, and Kd proportionally via ratio = αnom / α̂ (dashed line), maintaining consistent closed-loop performance without manual retuning.
